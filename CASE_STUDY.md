# AI Governance and Risk Management | Historical Case Study

**Portfolio case study | Jamal Davison**

> **Evidence boundary:** The artifacts are proposed models. No internal audit, deployment, operating-effectiveness test, or independently verified organizational outcome occurred.

## 1. Scenario
An independent review of public information about the historical Rite Aid facial-recognition matter, followed by a proposed AI governance program.

## 2. My role and contribution
Developed educational risk and governance artifacts. No affiliation with Rite Aid or access to its internal systems.

## 3. What I produced
- [Governance gap assessment](01_Gap_Assessment/)
- [Risk register](02_Risk_Register/)
- [Vendor review](06_Vendor_Review/)
- [Controls and oversight](07_Controls_and_Oversight/)
- [Implementation roadmap](09_Implementation_Roadmap/)

## 4. Decisions and reasoning
Organized nine workstreams around intake, risk assessment, accountability, third-party review, oversight, incident response, and staged adoption.

## 5. Results and verification
The linked deliverables are available for review in this repository. The artifacts are proposed models. No internal audit, deployment, operating-effectiveness test, or independently verified organizational outcome occurred.

## 6. What I would do next
With real organizational authorization, validate scope and assumptions with stakeholders, collect actual evidence, run appropriate tests, document exceptions, and seek accountable approval before any production rollout or closure claim.

## 7. Skills demonstrated
Security governance, risk assessment, evidence traceability, clear documentation, stakeholder-ready reporting, and separating proposed controls from verified results.
